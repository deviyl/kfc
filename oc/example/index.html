Before you join a role

  1. Look at your pass rate on an empty role. Must be 65 or higher. Press Join.
  2. If you have the weights userscript, check the role's weight. Under 10% means anyone can join.
  3. Nothing fits? Try a lower level crime, or ask leadership to spawn one for you.

Download the Torn OC Weights Under Roles userscript to view the individual role weights.
