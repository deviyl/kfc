Organized Crime Rules

OC Rules

Joining an Organized Crime is incredibly easy. It is a no-brainer for earning extra cash and respect for the faction for doing literally nothing except pressing "Join" on the right crime/role. Here are a few simple rules for our crimes at KFC you should follow:

  1. Join! This is a simple one, but after every recruitment drive, we have some members who just refuse to join an organized crime at all.

    AlertsYour name will appear in the alerts if you have not been in a crime for over 24 hours, and get an Overdue tag if you have not been in a crime in over 3 days.

  2. Level 1 and 2 crimes don't have any rules. These are spawned for new members who are at lower crime experience. Everyone has to start somewhere. Do whatever you want here. This is your playground to learn. If you are joining and you see someone in a role they have a super low pass rate paired with a super high role weight, coordinate among yourselves and fix it.

    Take some initiative here and learn the ropes of OCs.

    AlertsIf you are in any role in a level 1 or 2 crime, your name should never appear in any alerts.

  3. Check the role weights! At KFC, we have an easy rule to keep your name out of the alerts. Unless a role is under 10% weight (marked as a Freebie), you must have a pass rate of 65 at the minimum. For better success rates, we increase these pass rates as the role weight increases.

    1. Minimum of 65.
    2. If the weight is above 20%, you must have a 70 pass rate.
    3. If the weight is above 33%, you must have a 75 pass rate.

    These are for everyone to have the best success rate at passing the crime (don't just think of yourself-- you have teammates in these crimes). If you don't meet the requirements, you don't need to try to squeak in thinking "oh, i'm 64 pass rate. it will be fine" or "I'm 73 pass rate in this role with a weight of 44%. no worries." No. Remember, kids. This is about everyone. Your low pass rate could cost the whole operation their extra money. There are always lower crimes available to join. If you're not ready, there's no shame in that. Join a role suited for your pass rate.

    Alerts You will get an alert if you are under the required pass rate for any role you have joined. If you see your name in one of these alerts. Do everyone a favor. Drop the role, and pick one you qualify for.

  4. Remember. This is Torn. RNG is real. There is a 42.67% chance that none of this matters. There may be crimes where everyone has 90 pass rates that still fail. There may be crimes that slip by with someone at a 10 pass rate. Blame RNG. We just try to do what is best to maximize the chance of success.

    42.67% of all statistics are made up on the spot.

Organized Crime Alerts

Notes

At KFC, we have a Dicktator running who automatically monitors all of the crimes, their pass rates, missing items, delays, etc. You will get an alert in the discord channel #kfc-oc-dicktator every 6 hours. You should never find your name in this list. If the Dicktator has called you out-- you are doing something wrong.

Not in an OC

One of the easiest alerts for you to fix! Join an OC!

.

AlertsYou will get this alert if you have not been in an OC for over 24 hours.

Pass Rate Below Threshold

This one is also pretty easy to resolve all on your own. Join crimes where your pass rate meets the requirements for everyone to have the best success rate.

.

Minimum pass rate is 65 for all crimes unless the weight is below 10%, then we don't care. (Or if you're in a level 1/2 crime as mentioned above).

If your role's weight is greater than 20% importance-- you need a pass rate of 70 instead of 65.

If your role's weight is greater than 33% importance-- you need a pass rate of 75 instead of 65/70.

AlertsYou will get this alert if your pass rate is below the requirement.

Missing Required Item

This one is still easy, but could b e costly depdnding on the item. Before you join a role, if you hover the little progress dot, it will tell you what item your role requires. All you need to do is have it in your inventory.

.

AlertsYou will get this alert if you are missing the required item for your role.

Waiting on Member

This one requires a little planning on your part. You need to be "Okay" and in Torn when your crime completes or it will not complete.

.

If you are traveling, abroad, in the hospital, or jail.. your entire team has to wait for you to get back before they get paid and can join a new crime. Don't do it. Repeat offenders will have their OC income taken away and disbursed to the remaining members in your crime to compensate them for waiting.

.

Before you travel, you will get a warning in Torn: "Are you sure you want to travel? Your Organized Crime is xyz hours from completion."

AlertsYou will get this alert if a crime is ready to complete, but is waiting on YOU.
